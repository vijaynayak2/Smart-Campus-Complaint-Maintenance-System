How CampusFix Solves Infrastructure Problems

A strictly enforced lifecycle guaranteeing swift, accountable maintenance.

1. Report Issue

Students and staff submit complaints with category, precise campus location, urgency, and photo proof.

2. Admin Triage

Admin evaluates the request, adjusts priority level, and routes the complaint to specialized maintenance technicians.

3. Physical Repair

Assigned staff marks work 'In Progress', completes the physical repair, and uploads proof-of-completion photos.

4. Verification

Estate administrator audits repair quality on site and marks it 'Verified', locking the resolved case with full audit logs.
